**Overview:**
This project analyzes sales data to understand overall sales performance and identify important business insights.
The analysis focuses on **revenue trends, product performance, sales by city, and hourly sales patterns** using Python.

**Dataset:**
The dataset contains **185,950 sales records** covering multiple products and cities.

**Key Analysis**
- Overall revenue analysis
- Monthly sales and revenue trends
- Product-wise revenue comparison
- City-wise revenue analysis
- Hourly sales analysis
- Average quantity ordered

**Key Insights**
- The dataset contains 185,950 sales records.
- The overall revenue generated is approximately $34.49 million.
- There are 19 different products in the dataset.
- Revenue varies significantly across products.
- Los Angeles is one of the major revenue-generating cities, contributing approximately 15.81% of total revenue in the available city analysis.
- Boston contributes approximately 10.62% of total revenue.
- The average quantity ordered per sales record is approximately 1.12 units.
- Revenue was analyzed across different hours to identify hourly sales patterns.
- Monthly, product-wise, hourly, and city-wise analysis provides different perspectives on sales performance.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

**Conclusion:**
This project provides insights into sales performance across different products, cities, months, and hours, helping identify patterns and areas
of stronger sales performance.
